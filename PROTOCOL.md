## Temporal fit, selection and geographic boundary

Flanders 2018-2019 supplies all training forecast-error labels. Flanders 2020 is development, reported as four fixed calendar-quarter blocks. Select the candidate on equal-quarter mean normalized proxy cost at penalty ratio5and cap ratio0.1. Exact ties prefer the lower candidate index. Do not use any 2021-2023 error in fitting, preprocessing, scale setting or selection. Flanders 2021-2023 is temporal held evaluation. Wallonia 2021-2023 is geographic outcome transfer: no Wallonia error labels are used in training or selection. Its 2018-2019 monitored-capacity values alone define an earlier physical scale; this is capacity covariate adaptation, not an entirely unseen-domain claim. Export unused Wallonia 2018-2020 eligibility as unscored; do not inspect its error performance.

For each region define fixed scale S as the ordinary exact median of its eligible hourly monitored capacities during 2018-2019. Require S>0. Report S with units. This is an earlier descriptive monitored-capacity reference, not current available reserve. Normalize training residuals e=(forecast-measured)/S_Flanders. Training errors remain signed; never replace negative errors with zero before fitting a mean or quantile. Fit six ordered candidates:zero, mean, median, q75, q80, q90. Mean is the exact arithmetic mean; median is the ordinary midpoint median; quantiles use nearest rank x_(ceil(q*n)), one-based. These are established fixed estimators, not tuned ML. No random seed, feature search, parameter extension or forecast-model retraining.

For any region and scenario, book buffer r=S_region*min(c, max(0, b)), where b is the fitted normalized error statistic and c is cap ratio. Primary c=0.1;retain sensitivity c=0.05,0.1,0.2. This caps a hypothetical buffer at5%,10%or20%of an earlier monitored-capacity reference. It does not establish an engineering reserve limit, ramp/duration feasibility or a physical storage system.

Per eligible one-hour interval, uncovered shortfall u=max(forecast-measured-r,0), excess buffer=max(r-max(forecast-measured,0),0), and proxy loss L=r+lambda*u. Primary lambda=5;retain lambda=2,5,10 for each capacity ratio. The relative shortfall penalty is a declared scenario, not observed electricity prices, currency, reliability value or a calibrated utility cost. Report mean booked buffer, mean uncovered/excess MW, uncovered event frequency, coverage and mean L/S. Sums times1hour are energy-equivalent quantities, not measured reserve energy dispatch. A negative wind residual is surplus generation and needs no upward buffer under this one-sided proxy; downward balancing is outside scope.

Without a cap, the derivative of expected r+lambda*(e-r)_+ leads to the1-1/lambda residual quantile when regularity permits; lambda 5 motivates q80. This explains one candidate mathematically; it does not establish its held optimality. The finite six-rule set and capacity cap remain explicit. Select only on the primary development objective; all nine scenarios/all six policies remain visible in held results, no held-based policy change.

## Prior work and limits

Gneiting's Making and Evaluating Point Forecasts(arXiv0912.0902, primary author paper) explains why a forecast's functional and scoring rule must match. Quantile/asymmetric-loss policies are established; no novelty claim is made. This implementation asks whether a fixed practical buffer comparison transfers across the chosen dates/region. Reported upscaled production, source revisions, unknown issuance receipts, curtailment, capacity changes, calendar gaps and hypothetical penalties limit interpretation. No policy is validated for grid reliability or commercial operation. Independent program manuscripts may be public after checks; separate formal-publication projects remain private.
